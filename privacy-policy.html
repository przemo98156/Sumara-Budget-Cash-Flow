Privacy Policy

Mobile app “Sumara – Budget & Cash Flow” · Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Who is responsible for the app

The publisher of the app “Sumara – Budget & Cash Flow” (the “App”) is Przemysław Nowotniak (the “Publisher”). Contact for privacy matters: mobique.studio@gmail.com.

2. The key points in brief

  • Your financial data (accounts, transactions, categories, goals, descriptions and notes) is stored only on your device.
  • The Publisher has no access to it, does not send it to any server and does not require you to create an account.
  • The App contains no advertising, analytics tools or tracking mechanisms.
  • The only internet connection is used to download public exchange rates from the API of the National Bank of Poland (NBP).

3. What data the App processes

The App stores locally, in your device’s storage, the data that you enter yourself or import from a file:

  • account names, their currencies and balances;
  • transactions (amount, date, type, category, description, note);
  • categories and monthly limits (targets), as well as savings goals;
  • App settings (main currency, language, theme, and information that the guide shown on first launch has been displayed).

The App does not collect, among other things: your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier or data from other apps. It also does not require bank account or payment card numbers – account names are arbitrary labels, and the App does not connect to banks.

Because descriptions and notes are free-text fields, they may contain any information you type into them. It remains only on your device.

4. Internet connection – NBP exchange rates

To convert currencies, the App downloads public rates from the API of the National Bank of Poland (api.nbp.pl). Requests contain only the currency code and the date; they do not contain any of your financial data or personal data. Like any internet service, the NBP server technically sees the IP address of the device the request comes from – the rules for processing it are set by NBP (nbp.pl). Downloaded rates may be cached locally; without internet access the App uses cached or fallback rates.

5. Export, import and file sharing

  • Export (CSV, Excel) creates a file with your data only at your request. You can save it on your device or share it using the system share dialog (for example a cloud drive, email or a messenger).
  • Once you pass a file to another app or service, the data is subject to the privacy rules of that app or service – the Publisher has no influence over this and does not receive a copy of the file.
  • Import reads only the file that you choose yourself in the system file picker. The App does not browse other files on your device.

6. System backups

The App has no cloud backup feature of its own. However, Android and iOS may, in line with the settings of your device and account (for example Google Backup, iCloud), create copies of app data. Such copies are managed by the operating system provider, not by the Publisher. You can turn them off in your device settings.

7. Permissions

  • Internet access – solely to download NBP exchange rates.
  • File access is handled through the system file picker / file save dialog, without broad storage permissions.
  • The App does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts.

7a. No advertising or third-party services

As of the effective date of this policy, the App contains no advertising, analytics libraries or third-party error-reporting services that would collect data about the user. If this changes, the policy will be updated before any such change is introduced.

8. How long data is kept and how to delete it

Data remains on your device until you delete it. You can do this at any time:

  • in the App: Settings → Data → Delete all data (this operation cannot be undone – export your data first if you want to keep a copy);
  • by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in your system settings.

9. Your rights

Because the Publisher does not hold your data, it cannot provide, correct or delete it on your behalf – you have full control within the App itself. If you nevertheless believe that your personal data is being processed unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority – in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (uodo.gov.pl) – or with the authority in your own country of residence. You can also write to the address given in section 1 on privacy matters.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 16 years of age, and the Publisher does not knowingly collect any data from children.

11. Security

Data is stored in your device’s storage, in an area intended for the App and protected by the operating system. The App does not apply additional encryption to its database, so we recommend protecting your device with a screen lock and being careful when sharing export files, which contain data in plain form.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when adding new features. The current version is always available at https://przemo98156.github.io/Sumara-Budget-Cash-Flow/privacy-policy.html, and the date of the last change is shown at the top of the document.
